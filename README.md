# My Favorite Movie: *White Chicks*

## 📖 Overview
**Title:** White Chicks  
**Genre:** Comedy  
**Release Year:** 2004  
**Directed by:** Keenen Ivory Wayans  

*White Chicks* is a hilarious comedy about two FBI agents who go undercover as white socialite sisters to solve a kidnapping case. The film is full of outrageous humor, mistaken identities, and unforgettable quotes.

---

## 👩‍🦱 Main Characters
| Character | Actor | Description |
|------------|--------|-------------|
| Kevin Copeland | Shawn Wayans | One of the FBI agents who disguises himself as Brittany. |
| Marcus Copeland | Marlon Wayans | Kevin’s partner, who disguises himself as Tiffany. |
| Heather Vandergeld | Jaime King | A wealthy socialite caught up in the case. |
| Lisa Anderson | Busy Philipps | One of the socialite friends. |

---

## 🎭 Favorite Scene
> “Yo, hold my poodle! HOLD MY POODLE!”

This scene never fails to make me laugh — it perfectly captures the ridiculous and over-the-top humor that defines the movie.

---

## 💬 Why I Love It
- It’s **funny and lighthearted**
- The **disguises and transformations** are both creative and absurd
- The movie delivers **satire on class, race, and beauty standards**
- It’s the kind of movie that’s *so silly it’s iconic*

---

## ⭐ Rating
**My Rating:** ★★★★☆ (4.5/5)  
Because it’s the perfect comfort movie for when I need a laugh!
